[<img src="/assets/img/me/nikosme.png" height="auto" width="50%" ALIGN="right"/>](## "photo by Nikos Antonio Kourous Vázquez")

Kat Macdonald is a London-based sound and media artist exploring collective practice, embodiment and technology. Her work uses pedagogy, open scoring and collective activations as a method to draw attention to social systems and infrastructures that lie unacknowledged in everyday life. Through noticing these forces, we can then make decisions around how we orientate ourselves around them.

Making between disciplines, Kat works with sound, visuals, the web, sculptural work, choreography, scoring and performance, in addition to site-specific installations, often engaging with technology to critique the ways in which it can shape culture and practice.

She is the founder and organiser of the [London Community Laptop Orchestra][lclo] (2024 - ), an all-abilities, community-organised laptop collective and research project. The LCLO takes social/ technological theory and infrastructure, and represents them through collective participatory actions. The project is site specific, seeking to grow through 'branching' and deviation, rather than touring, borrowing from the principles of the decentralised web and 'open source' cultures. 

Her current practice explores inner city living and spaces of community, looking at how public space and micro publics (sites of everyday cultural exchange) play a role in holding and building communities and networks of care. Her work looks at how these spaces are changing in response to technology and privatisation, influencing our relationship to otherness.

Having received her MA in Experimental Music from Brunel University in 2018, she went on to work as the resident sound and sensor artist for the [Design And Performance Lab][dap] between 2017 - 2022, under the direction of [Johannes Birringer][johan] and [Michèle Danjoux][danjoux], designing custom sound and visual software and working with wearable sensors. During this time a core concern of her practice was generative and indeterminate compositions, relying on live-data to create free-form sonic environments.

She has worked as an artist, a technician, a chef, a graphic designer, a web designer, a sales assistant, a social media manager, a bartender, an art handler, a kitchen porter, a communications manager, in IT, in convenience stores, in cafes, in galleries, in nightclubs, in advertising, in offices, out of offices, in the field, in the studio, in the garden and internationally.

Previously know or made work under the following names: Dee Kathleen Egan, A-Kin, <s>Drew Egan</s> and <s>Andrew Egan</s>.
